# 🚀 Zest Academy — Learn Engineering. Build the Future.

**Zest Academy** is an open, community-driven learning platform built to help engineering students master core concepts, modern technologies, and real-world skills — all in one place.

> From fundamentals to industry-grade projects, Zest Academy is designed to convert learners into builders.

---

## 🎯 Mission

To provide **structured, practical, and industry-relevant education** that empowers engineering students to become confident problem-solvers and innovators.

---

## 🧠 What Zest Academy Offers

### 📚 Learning Resources
- Core engineering concepts (EEE, ECE, CSE, IT, MECH, CIVIL & more)
- Programming fundamentals & advanced topics
- Interview preparation materials
- Career guidance & roadmaps

### 🛠️ Skill-Building Content
- Project-based learning modules  
- Real-world case studies  
- Hands-on labs & challenges

### 💼 Career Preparation
- Interview questions & mock challenges
- Resume & portfolio guidance
- Industry-oriented learning paths
